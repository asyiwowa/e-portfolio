Career Exploration

I am interested in pursuing a career as a Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst. A SOC Analyst is responsible for monitoring network activities, detecting cyber threats, investigating suspicious activities, and responding to cybersecurity incidents.


This role is highly relevant in modern cybersecurity because organizations today face increasing cyber threats such as ransomware, phishing attacks, malware, and data breaches. SOC Analysts help organizations maintain system security, protect sensitive information, and reduce cybersecurity risks.
